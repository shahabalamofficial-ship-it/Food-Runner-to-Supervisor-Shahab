# Day 1 — Professional Identity: Food Runner

## Today's outcome
Understand what a reliable Food Runner is responsible for and how to communicate without pretending to know something.

## Key principle
**English:** My responsibility is to deliver the correct dish to the correct guest, safely and politely.
**اردو:** میری ذمہ داری ہے کہ صحیح کھانا صحیح مہمان تک، محفوظ اور باادب طریقے سے پہنچاؤں۔

## Before carrying a dish
- Confirm the dish name.
- Confirm table number and seat/guest position according to the restaurant's system.
- Check visible presentation and required accompaniments against the ticket/SOP.
- If anything is unclear, ask the pass/kitchen or captain. Never guess.
- Carry safely and avoid reaching across a guest where possible.

## Guest phrase
**English:** “Excuse me. May I place this here?”
**اردو:** “معاف کیجیے گا، کیا میں یہ پلیٹ یہاں رکھ سکتا ہوں؟”

**English:** “This is your [dish name]. Please enjoy.”
**اردو:** “یہ آپ کی [ڈش کا نام] ہے۔ نوش فرمائیے۔”

## If you do not know the answer
**English:** “I want to make sure I give you the correct information. Let me confirm that with the kitchen.”
**اردو:** “میں چاہتا ہوں کہ آپ کو درست معلومات دوں۔ میں کچن سے تصدیق کر کے بتاتا ہوں۔”

## Role-play
A guest asks whether a dish contains nuts. You are not certain.
Correct response: do not guess, do not say “it's safe”. Tell the guest you will verify, inform the captain and kitchen, and follow the restaurant's allergy procedure.

## Mastery check
Answer without looking:
1. What are the three checks before delivering a dish?
2. What do you say when you need to confirm an ingredient?
3. Why must you never guess about allergens?

**Pass rule:** 3/3 correct, and a spoken role-play with no unsafe promise. If not passed, repeat this lesson.
