# 30-Day Roadmap — Food Runner to Supervisor

This is a structured foundation plan, not a promise of promotion in 30 days. Real promotion requires consistent on-shift performance, manager feedback, experience, and demonstrated leadership. If a lesson is not mastered, repeat it before moving forward; the calendar can stretch.

| Days | Stage | Main outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Food Runner foundations | Role clarity, restaurant layout, communication, safety |
| 4–7 | Sequence of Service | Welcome, first contact, drinks, order flow, food delivery, clearing, farewell |
| 8–14 | Menu Product Knowledge I | One verified dish at a time; ingredients, taste only if confirmed, guest explanation |
| 15–18 | Menu Product Knowledge II | Main courses, sides, sauces, beverage knowledge, dish comparisons |
| 19–21 | Guest Confidence | Eye contact, calm voice, English recovery phrases, VIP mindset |
| 22–24 | Family Table Mastery | Seat mapping, correct dish-to-guest delivery, safe plate placement, coordinated serving |
| 25–27 | Waiter / Senior Runner skills | Order accuracy, follow-up, upselling only when appropriate, complaint recovery |
| 28–29 | Captain foundations | Briefing, section control, team coordination, service checks |
| 30 | Supervisor foundations + assessment | Delegation, handover, problem escalation, practical final assessment |

## Daily lesson gate
A lesson is complete only when Shahab can:
1. Explain the concept in his own words.
2. Perform the guest-facing script without reading.
3. Apply it in a realistic role-play.
4. Score at least 80% on the recall check.
5. Identify when to ask a manager/kitchen instead of guessing.

If any critical safety or order-accuracy point is missed, the lesson is **not passed**, even if the total score is high.

## Daily study session (40–50 minutes)
1. Revision — 10 minutes.
2. New topic — 15 minutes.
3. Spoken practice — 10 minutes.
4. Scenario practice — 10 minutes.
5. Test and progress log — 5 minutes.
