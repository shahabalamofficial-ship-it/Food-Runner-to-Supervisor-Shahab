# Family Table Mastery

## Main risk
The common failure is not simply dropping a plate. It is delivering the wrong dish to the wrong person, interrupting the family, reaching across someone, or losing track of modifications/allergies.

## Before leaving the pass
1. Confirm table number.
2. Confirm each dish and its modifiers.
3. Use the restaurant's seat-numbering system if available.
4. If there is no reliable seat map, ask the captain or use a polite confirmation at the table.
5. Never identify a dish by guessing from appearance.

## At the table
- Approach from the safest, least disruptive side according to restaurant training.
- Do not reach across a guest if another safe route is available.
- Ask permission when needed: “Excuse me, may I place this here?”
- Announce the exact dish name.
- Confirm the correct guest discreetly if uncertain: “May I confirm, is this the grilled [dish] for you?”
- Be especially careful with allergy and special-request dishes.
- Do not rush or pass hot plates over a child or another guest.

## Example: family of four
Before service, mentally map: Seat 1 — dish A; Seat 2 — dish B; Seat 3 — dish C; Seat 4 — dish D. This is only a practice example. Use the restaurant's actual seat numbering and POS tickets on shift.

## Recovery if you make a mistake
1. Stop; do not pretend it did not happen.
2. Apologize briefly and respectfully.
3. Do not move food between guests or plates without permission and policy.
4. Inform the captain immediately.
5. Follow the captain's instruction and correct the delivery.
6. Record or report the issue according to restaurant procedure.

## Guest phrases
**English:** “Excuse me, may I confirm who ordered the [dish name]?”
**اردو:** “معاف کیجیے گا، کیا میں تصدیق کر سکتا ہوں کہ [ڈش کا نام] کس نے آرڈر کی تھی؟”

**English:** “I apologize. Let me correct that for you.”
**اردو:** “میں معذرت چاہتا ہوں۔ میں ابھی اسے درست کرواتا ہوں۔”
